Fresh harvests, rather than shop availability

A crop appears when growers in France normally harvest it during that month. The calendar excludes imports and produce available only from long-term storage, heated greenhouses, or other protected methods.

Locally grown produce sold outside these harvest months may have been stored after harvest. A blank fruit or vegetable month does not mean shops have no locally grown produce.

This is a selected catalogue of common fruit and vegetables, not a complete list of every crop in France. Herbs, mushrooms, nuts and rhubarb are outside its scope. Tomatoes, peppers and cucumbers are grouped with vegetables.

Seasons vary across France

This is an approximate national guide, not a local crop forecast. Weather, latitude, altitude, cultivar, and farming method can shift harvest dates by several weeks. Harvests may begin earlier in warmer areas and later in cooler areas.

ADEME does not mark stored produce. For apples, pears, carrots, beetroot, celeriac, turnips, pumpkins and squash, the catalogue keeps months within the Belgium and northern France fresh-harvest windows. This northern overlap is conservative for warmer French regions. It omits onions, imported citrus and tropical fruit, and forced chicory.

Use the calendar to plan seasonal meals, then check what's being harvested by growers near you. The month ranges describe typical seasons rather than a prediction for this year's weather.
