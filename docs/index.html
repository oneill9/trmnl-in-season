What “in season” means

An item appears when it is commonly harvested domestically during the selected calendar month. Imports and produce available only from long-term storage, heated greenhouses, or other protected growing are excluded.

Government bodies, national horticultural organisations, and national produce boards are preferred. Where no sufficiently detailed national source exists, the guide keeps the conservative overlap between two reputable regional sources. Weather, latitude, altitude, cultivar, and farming method can shift real harvest dates by several weeks.
