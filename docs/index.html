What "in season" means

The guide includes a fruit or vegetable when growers normally harvest it in the selected country during that month. It excludes imports and produce available only from long-term storage, heated greenhouses, or other protected methods.

The guide uses government sources, national horticultural organisations, and national produce boards where possible. If no detailed national source exists, it uses the overlap between two reputable regional sources. Weather, latitude, altitude, cultivar, and farming method can shift harvest dates by several weeks.

United Kingdom

UK seasonal fruit and vegetable calendar: see fresh harvests for all twelve months.

  • Royal Horticultural Society, Grow Your Own, including its fruit gardening calendar, vegetable crop planner, and harvesting guidance.
  • Worcestershire County Council, How to eat seasonally.

Ireland

  • Bord Bia, Best in Season calendar.

Netherlands

  • Voedingscentrum, Wat zijn seizoensgroenten en seizoensfruit?, including its seasonal calendar.

The calendar does not mark stored produce. For apples, pears, onions, pumpkins, carrots, beetroot, celeriac and cabbage, the guide keeps only the months within the United Kingdom and Ireland fresh-harvest windows. Reviewed 28 September 2026.

Belgium

  • Brussels Environment, Calendrier des fruits & légumes locaux, de saison, et hors serre.

The calendar covers Belgium and neighbouring northern France, excludes heated greenhouses and marks stored produce separately. The guide keeps only its fresh months. Reviewed 29 September 2026.

France

  • ADEME Impact CO2, Fruits et légumes de saison.

The calendar does not mark stored produce. For apples, pears, carrots, beetroot, celeriac, turnips, pumpkins and squash, the guide keeps only the months within the Belgium and northern France fresh-harvest windows. It omits onions, citrus and tropical fruit. Reviewed 29 September 2026.

Germany

  • Verbraucherzentrale, Saisonkalender: Obst und Gemüse frisch und regional einkaufen, including its seasonal calendar.

The calendar separates open-field, foil-covered, greenhouse and stored produce. The guide keeps only open-field months, so it omits German tomatoes, peppers and aubergines. Reviewed 29 September 2026.

United States

  • USDA SNAP-Ed, Seasonal Produce Guide.

Canada

  • Foodland Ontario, Availability guide.
  • Buy BC, Seasonal chart.

When provincial calendars differ, the guide keeps only their overlapping fresh-harvest periods.

Australia

  • Sydney Local Health District, Seasonal Fruit and Vegetable Guide.
  • Brisbane City Council, Buying local, seasonal fruit and vegetables.

The guide keeps seasons shared by the temperate and subtropical sources.

New Zealand

  • Work and Income New Zealand, Fruit and vegetable seasonal work calendar.
  • Horticulture New Zealand, Vegetable seasonability chart.
