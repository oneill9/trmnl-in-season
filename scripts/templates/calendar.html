Fresh harvests, rather than shop availability

A crop appears when growers in {{LOCATION}} normally harvest it during that month. The calendar excludes imports and produce available only from long-term storage, heated greenhouses, or other protected methods.

{{AVAILABILITY_NOTE}}

This is a selected catalogue of common fruit and vegetables, not a complete list of every crop in {{LOCATION}}. Herbs, mushrooms, nuts and rhubarb are outside its scope. Tomatoes, peppers and cucumbers are grouped with vegetables.

Seasons vary across {{LOCATION}}

This is an approximate national guide, not a local crop forecast. Weather, latitude, altitude, cultivar, and farming method can shift harvest dates by several weeks. Harvests may begin earlier in warmer areas and later in cooler areas.

{{REGIONAL_NOTE}}

Use the calendar to plan seasonal meals, then check what's being harvested by growers near you. The month ranges describe typical seasons rather than a prediction for this year's weather.
